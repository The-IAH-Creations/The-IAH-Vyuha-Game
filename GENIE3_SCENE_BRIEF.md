# Authorized Genie 3 scene brief - The IAH Vyuha v2.0.0

Purpose: generate an explorable Hampi-inspired environment for an authorized Genie 3 workflow, with district-specific spaces designed around the updated game loop. Keep gameplay collision geometry and objective coordinates separate from generated visuals so the scene can be swapped without changing game logic.

## Core scene prompt
Create a navigable first-person environment inspired by the rocky landscape around Hampi, Karnataka, India, using a respectful fictionalized presentation rather than a documentary reconstruction. Build one connected resonance frontier with five visibly distinct districts:

1. **Sunken Mandala Court** - stepped granite, circular/mandala-like floor traces, covered lanes, resonance pylons and a partially buried gate.
2. **Whisper Bazaar** - irregular market canopies, broken arcades, narrow sightlines, hanging fabrics and reflective stone surfaces that support ambush gameplay.
3. **Sky-Step Observatory** - dark-stone terraces, elevated steps, a lens/telescope monument, long sightlines and safe/unsafe cover pockets.
4. **River Memory Court** - dry river channels, mist pockets, water-marked stones, reflective pools and moving resonance traces.
5. **Naga Crucible** - a subterranean-feeling arena cut into stone, ring-like resonance architecture and a central boss altar.

Use granite boulder fields, dry stone paths, palms, scrub, stepped stone walls, scattered market structures, warm late-afternoon light, seasonal river haze, and subtle bioluminescent resonance traces. Make traversal lanes, cover pockets, doorways, courtyards, alternate routes and boss space easy to read.

## Enemy readability
Create enough lighting contrast for human-like silhouettes to read clearly at medium distance. Do not create identifiable real people. Wardens should appear as fictional cloaked/masked humanoid guardians with readable posture and weapon telegraphs, not as geometric targets.

## Art direction
Photoreal textures, grounded materials, physically plausible lighting, atmospheric depth, restrained fantasy effects, tactically readable silhouettes, and respectful fictional ancient-India-inspired motifs. Avoid copyrighted logos, modern advertising, private-property details, or documentary reconstruction claims.

## Integration
Export or stream the generated environment through the authorized Genie 3 workflow. Keep coordinate anchors stable for:
- Virupaksha Echo Gate / checkpoint
- Mandala Wellspring
- Whisper Bazaar Loom
- Sky-Step Lens
- River Memory Court
- Naga Crucible

The browser prototype can continue to use its lightweight procedural renderer when the generated 3D scene is unavailable/offline.
