# Third-party and asset policy

The core prototype is authored specifically for this deliverable and uses no external runtime game-library dependency.

Google Maps / Satellite: do not copy, scrape, bundle, or redistribute Google imagery in the offline package. A commercial online edition that uses Google Maps Platform should follow the applicable Google Maps Platform agreement and attribution requirements.

Genie 3: only use outputs obtained through an authorized Google workflow and follow the terms that apply to that workflow.

Audio: the prototype generates its BGM/SFX at runtime through WebAudio; no third-party music files are included.
